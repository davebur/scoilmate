## Proposed learning sequence (editorial, not mandated)
- **English:** Y1 figurative language, literal detail/inference using original short passages, purpose/audience, drafting; Y2 compare viewpoints, genre and evidence in responses; Y3 integrate analysis and revision with school-selected texts. Initial topics: figurative language (candidate Reading 8), purpose/audience (candidate Writing 4/12), evidence-led reading. Oral-language tips may support preparation but MCQs do not assess speaking. Map exact outcomes after educator review.
- **Gaeilge T2:** Y1 short practical messages, time/place details, school/day-to-day vocabulary in context; Y2 connected descriptions and language/cultural awareness; Y3 interpreting texts and checking/revising communication. Teach language in purposeful contexts, not only grammar drills. Listening and spoken interaction need later original reviewed audio/speaking activities; text MCQs cannot claim this coverage.
- **Gaeilge T1:** richer contextual reading, audience/register, critical response and cultural/language awareness; progressively more nuanced original texts and reflection. Native/Irish-medium speakers need appropriate challenge, not merely relabelled T2 exercises. Review all Irish and translations, dialect/register, fada rendering and pedagogical assumptions with competent Gaeilge educators.
- **History:** Y1 introduce chronology/evidence within selected Irish and wider-world contexts; Y2 corroboration, causation, change/continuity in further contexts; Y3 compare interpretations and construct supported judgements. Initial topics: source origin/usefulness (candidate 1.6), historical concepts (1.4), evidence-led judgements (1.7); add reviewed contextual topics from strands 2/3 rather than a skills-only bank. Do not equate primary with truthful or biased with useless.

## Editorial pipeline
Author original short lesson and MCQ → check one best answer and plausible non-trick distractors → map outcome/release/track/year/level → record sources and rights/provenance → subject and language review → accessibility/safeguarding review → publish immutable version. Prefer independent reviewer; if unavailable, do not label content reviewed. Retire factual errors promptly, notify affected students proportionately, preserve historical attempts with correction note.

Samples have three items per subject, two T2 and one T1 for Gaeilge. Every item has two hints, explanation and linked draft help; all exact outcome mappings remain candidate/unreviewed. They illustrate format, not approved first-year suitability or exam-standard difficulty. Sources inform curriculum planning; sample prompts/texts are newly authored, not copies/adaptations of SEC papers.
